## Recording tips

- Record in a quiet room, with your phone about 20 cm from your mouth.
- Keep the WiFi-off icon visible in at least one shot. The rules require the app to run with WiFi off.
- Label every sped-up section (for example "4x") so judges know it's real on-device work.
- The sample documents are fictional and marked SPECIMEN. Call them "sample documents".
